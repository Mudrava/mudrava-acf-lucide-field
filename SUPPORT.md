# Support

Mudrava Icon Field for ACF with Lucide is free and open source. Support is
provided on a best-effort basis through these channels:

- **GitHub Discussions** (preferred): open a thread in the
  [Discussions](../../discussions) tab for questions and ideas.
- **WordPress.org support forum**:
  [wordpress.org/support/plugin/mudrava-acf-lucide-field](https://wordpress.org/support/plugin/mudrava-acf-lucide-field/)
- **Email:** [support@mudrava.com](mailto:support@mudrava.com) for anything
  private.
- **Security issues:** do not open a public issue. Follow
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Before you open a ticket

1. Confirm you run ACF (free or Pro) 6.0 or newer - the field type will not
   register on older ACF.
2. Update the plugin, WordPress and PHP to current versions.
3. Include: WordPress, PHP, ACF and plugin versions, plus the exact steps to
   reproduce.
4. For a missing icon, note the icon name and whether it is a Lucide or
   `simple:` brand icon.

## Response times

We aim to acknowledge threads and email within 2-3 business days. There is no
guaranteed support SLA for the free plugin.

## Commercial help

For custom field work or integration help, reach out at
[mudrava.com/en/](https://mudrava.com/en/).
